Badger Open in your browser

Browse without being seen.

Badger opens every site through its own server, so sites see the server's address, not yours. Ads and trackers are blocked, every site is walled off from the others, and nothing about you is kept on the server.

Download for Windows Use it on iPhone or any browser

Windows 10 and 11 · Free and open source

Your address stays hidden

Every page loads through Badger's server. For secure sites, the encryption happens on your device, so the server only passes along scrambled data.

Ads and trackers blocked

uBlock Origin's lists block ads, trackers and known phishing and malware sites, with a warning before anything dangerous opens.

Every site walled off

Each site runs on its own address, so a bad site can't reach the logins or data of the others.

Nothing kept on the server

No accounts, no database, no logs of where you go. History, bookmarks and logins stay on your device.

Locked with a passphrase

Optionally encrypt your history, bookmarks and tabs on the device. By default, site logins are cleared each time Badger opens.

No pop-ups, no surprises

Pages can't open windows, show dialogs or hand you off to other apps without a tap. WebRTC is off, so your real address can't leak.

On iPhone

  1. Open browse.badgerbrowser.com in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Badger from its icon on your home screen.

On Windows

  1. Download and run Badger-Setup.exe.
  2. If Windows SmartScreen warns you, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Badger keeps itself up to date.
