Terms and privacy

Short version: use it like any browser, don't use it to hurt anyone, and we don't keep your data.

What this is

A free web browser that runs through this server. Pages you open are fetched through it, with ads, trackers and pop-ups blocked. It's offered as is, with no guarantee that it works, stays available, or that every site loads.

Privacy

  • There are no accounts and no database. We don't record or sell the sites you visit.
  • Your logins, cookies and site data stay on your device. So do your history, bookmarks, open tabs and the count of blocked trackers; none of it is sent to the server. Clear history and site data anytime in Settings with Clear history and site data now (bookmarks are kept).
  • For secure (https://) sites, encryption happens on your device. The server only passes along encrypted data and can't read your passwords or the pages you see.
  • The server does see the name of each site you open (like example.com), to check it against lists of malware and phishing sites. It isn't logged or kept.
  • Some videos are fetched by the server directly for your device's video player. The hosting provider's request logs may record those video addresses.
  • Your settings are kept in a cookie on your device. A second cookie shows your device passed the automatic bot check; it expires after 30 days.

Traffic is limited per connection and per day so the service stays fair and affordable. Access may be refused to anyone who misuses it.
